Where to get the official data
  1. STK-7 register workbook (this project): one sheet of struck-off companies from RoC and C-PACE notices, 2017 to 2026, with CIN confidence and source page.
  2. MCA company master data on the Open Government Data platform: search data.gov.in “Company Master Data”. It carries CIN, name, status, RoC and registration date.
  3. Confirm any hit on the MCA portal's View Company/LLP Master Data before you act on it.
