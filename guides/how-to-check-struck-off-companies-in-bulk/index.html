How to check struck-off companies in bulk

Checking vendors one by one on the MCA portal doesn't scale. Here's how to screen a whole vendor or customer list in one go, and what to do with the results.

Why check in bulk

Auditors and finance teams usually need to know whether any vendor, customer or investee in a list of hundreds or thousands has been struck off: for the Schedule III disclosure of relationships with struck-off companies, for vendor onboarding and KYC, or before a payment run. The MCA portal lets you look up one company at a time, which is fine for a handful of names but not for a full vendor master.

Step 1: export your list

You need one row per party with the name, and the CIN if you have it. Common sources:

  • Tally: open the list of ledgers under Sundry Creditors (vendors) or Sundry Debtors (customers) and export it to Excel.
  • SAP, Oracle, Zoho, Busy or another ERP: export the vendor or customer master report to Excel or CSV.
  • Investments and shareholders: the investment register and the register of members.

Keep the original column headings; you don't need to tidy the names. "ABC Pvt. Ltd.", "ABC Private Limited" and "M/s ABC P Ltd" are treated as the same company when matching.

Step 2: match against the struck-off register

Option A: Struck-Off Company Check (fastest)

  1. Create a free account and sign in.
  2. Open Check a vendor / customer list and choose your Excel or CSV file, or paste names one per line.
  3. The portal finds the name and CIN columns itself (you can change them) and checks every row against 9,65,508 struck-off companies and LLPs.
  4. Download the results to Excel for your working papers.

Your file is read in your browser and is never uploaded to our servers.

Option B: do it yourself in Excel

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs publishes company master data on data.gov.in, with a status for every company. You can download it, filter CompanyStatus = Strike Off, and use XLOOKUP or VLOOKUP on CIN against your list. This works well if every party has a CIN. It is harder when you only have names: names in your ledgers rarely match the MCA spelling exactly, and the master data does not show the strike-off date or notice.

Step 3: read the results

ResultWhat to do
Struck off, CIN matchCertain match. Record it and follow up.
Struck off, exact nameSame name after removing "Pvt Ltd", punctuation and similar words. Confirm the CIN with the vendor or on the MCA portal.

Not found in registerNot in the struck-off register. It may still be dormant, under liquidation or amalgamated, so check high-value parties on the MCA portal.

Step 4: act and document

  • Block struck-off vendors in the ERP and pause payments until the position is clear. Dealing with struck-off vendors and customers →
  • For year-end reporting, record balances and transactions with each struck-off company for the Schedule III table.
  • Keep the exported results, with the notice number and source for each match, as audit evidence.
  • Repeat the check at least at each year end, and before onboarding new vendors. New STK-7 notices are published every few weeks.

Tips for better matches

  • Add CINs wherever you can. A CIN match is certain; a name match needs a quick review.
  • Leave out individuals, partnership firms and proprietorships. Only companies and LLPs can be struck off by the RoC; other parties will simply show as not found.
  • Check former names. If a vendor changed its name, the notice may list the old one.
