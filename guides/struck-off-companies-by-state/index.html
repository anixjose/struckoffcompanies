Struck-off companies in India by state and year

Counts of struck-off companies and LLPs in each state and union territory, and of STK-7 notices by year, from the Struck-Off Company Check register.

The Struck-Off Company Check register holds 9,62,219 struck-off companies and LLPs. 2,28,453 come from the STK-7 notices and lists published by the Registrars of Companies and C-PACE since 2017; the rest are companies and LLPs marked "Strike Off" in the Ministry of Corporate Affairs' company master data, including strike-offs from before 2017.
