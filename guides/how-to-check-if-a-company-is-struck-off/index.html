How to check if a company is struck off in India

Four checks that tell you whether a vendor or customer still exists as a company, and how to document the result for audit.

1. Find the company's CIN

Every company registered in India has a 21-character Corporate Identification Number (CIN), for example U74999MH2015PTC123456. LLPs have an LLPIN such as AAB-1234. You'll usually find it on:

  • the vendor's invoice, letterhead or website footer (companies must print their CIN under section 12(3)(c) of the Companies Act, 2013);
  • the vendor master in your ERP or Tally, or the vendor registration form;
  • the MCA portal, by searching the company name.

Searching by CIN is more reliable than by name: names change, and many companies have near-identical names.

2. Check the status on the MCA portal

On the Ministry of Corporate Affairs website, open MCA Services → Master Data → View Company or LLP Master Data, enter the CIN or name, and read the Company Status field.

StatusWhat it means
ActiveThe company exists and is not in strike-off or winding-up proceedings.
Strike OffThe Registrar of Companies (RoC) has removed the name from the register under section 248. The company stands dissolved.
Under process of Striking OffA strike-off notice (STK-5 or STK-6) has been published; the company may be struck off soon.
Amalgamated, Dissolved, Under Liquidation, Under CIRPOther ways a company stops trading normally. Treat these as red flags too.
Dormant (s.455)The company has asked to be treated as inactive. It exists but should not be trading.

3. Read the STK-7 notice

When the RoC strikes a company off, it publishes a notice in Form STK-7 in the Official Gazette, listing every company removed. The notice gives the date the company was dissolved and whether it was struck off by the RoC on its own (section 248(1)) or at the company's request (section 248(2)). Since May 2023, voluntary strike-offs are processed by the Centre for Processing Accelerated Corporate Exit (C-PACE), which issues its own STK-7 notices.

The notice is your audit evidence: keep its number, date and the page that lists the company. How the section 248 process works →

4. Make sure it hasn't been restored

A struck-off company can be restored by the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) under section 252, up to 20 years after the STK-7 notice. Once the RoC records the NCLT order, the MCA status changes back to Active. So if a company appears in an STK-7 notice but MCA now shows it as Active, check whether it was restored before treating it as struck off. Restoration under section 252 →

What to do if a vendor is struck off

Stop new orders and payments until you understand the position, and ask the vendor for their NCLT restoration order if they say they are still trading.
